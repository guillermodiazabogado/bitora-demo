# BITORA V4.7 Functional Scope

V4.7 provides a foundation for operational history and autocomplete:

- Event timeline from `audit_logs`.
- Entity timeline for event-scoped entities.
- Participant autocomplete scoped by organization and optionally by event.
- Speaker autocomplete scoped by organization.
- Reusable value autocomplete for institutions, cities and roles.
- Duplicate candidate search by email, document and normalized name.
- Confirm/dismiss duplicate decisions with audit.

Out of scope:
- Replacing or rewriting audit.
- Automatic merges of people.
- Free access to private data.
- Cross-tenant search.
- Product flows for V4.8 or later.
